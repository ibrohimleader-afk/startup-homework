How to do a Proper Squat

Follow this step-by-step guide to execute the exercise safely and effectively. Estimated execution time: 5 minutes.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Stand straight with your feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Tighten your stomach muscles and keep your chest up.
  3. Lower your hips as if you were sitting down on an invisible chair.
  4. Keep your knees over your ankles and don't let them move inward.
  5. Push back up to the starting position through your heels.
