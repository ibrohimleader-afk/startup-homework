Exercise List

Exercise Name Muscle Group Reps / Sets
Squats Keep your feet shoulder-width apart, lower your hips until thighs are parallel to the floor, and push back up through your heels. Quads, Glutes & Hamstrings 3 x 12 reps
Push-ups Maintain a rigid plank position, lower your chest until it nearly touches the ground, then drive upwards keeping elbows stable. Chest, Triceps & Anterior Deltoids 4 x 15 reps
Pull-ups Hang from a bar with an overhand grip, pull your body weight up until your chin clears the bar, and lower yourself under control. Lats, Rhomboids & Upper Back 3 x 8 reps
Plank Support your body weight on your forearms and toes, squeezing your glutes and core tightly without letting your lower back sag. Core, Transverse Abdominis & Lower Back 3 x 60 seconds
Dips Lower your body using parallel bars until your shoulders are below your elbows, then press back up explosively. Lower Chest & Triceps Brachii 3 x 10 reps
Lunges Step forward with one leg, lowering your hips until both knees are bent at a 90-degree angle, then return to the starting stance. Quadriceps & Calf Muscles 3 x 15 reps
