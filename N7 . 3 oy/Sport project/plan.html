Fitness & Sport Program

Weekly Workout Plan

Day Activity / Exercises Duration
Monday Leg Day & Lower Body Strength: Heavy bodyweight squats, walking lunges, calf raises, and glute bridges. High-intensity finish with jump squats. 1 hour 15 mins
Tuesday Cardio Endurance & Core Power: 5km outdoor running, followed by dynamic core intervals including forearms plank, side planks, bicycle crunches, and mountain climbers. 55 mins
Wednesday Active Recovery & Rest Day: Complete muscle relaxation. No heavy lifting. Includes a 30-minute light walk in nature and deep breathing hydration routine. —
Thursday Chest, Triceps & Shoulders: Classic push-ups, diamond push-ups, bench dips, overhead dumbbell press alternatives, and incline pushing movements to target upper chest. 1 hour
Friday Back, Biceps & Pull-ups focus: Standard dead-hang pull-ups, chin-ups for biceps isolation, bodyweight inverted rows, and lower back extensions. 1 hour 10 mins
Saturday Full Body Flexibility & Mobility: Hatha Yoga flow, dynamic stretching sequence to open tight hips, hamstring elongation, and full-body foam rolling session. 45 mins
Sunday Rest Day: Complete physiological rejuvenation. Focus on macro-nutrition intake tracking, mental wellness, and strategic planning for the upcoming training week. —
